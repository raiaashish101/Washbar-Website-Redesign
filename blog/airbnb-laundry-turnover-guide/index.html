Airbnb & Short Stay

A practical laundry turnover guide for Airbnb hosts

A repeatable linen plan can help hosts, short-stay operators and property managers work through the limited window between guests.

Wash Bar Team25 September 2026

For a short-stay property, laundry can become one of the biggest bottlenecks between checkout and the next arrival. Same-day turnovers can leave only a limited window, so the useful question is not just how to wash the linen, but how the whole process will keep moving when plans change.

Start with the actual turnover load

List what must be ready for each stay: sheets, pillowcases, towels, bath mats and any other washable guest items. Record the quantities, sizes and care instructions so the person handling the turnover does not have to make those decisions from memory.

Build a repeatable linen routine

  • Keep spare sets. An extra set of bedding and towels gives the property a ready replacement while used linen is being washed.
  • Standardise where practical. Consistent sheet sizes, towel colours and storage labels make sorting and restocking clearer.
  • Use a checklist. Record the order for stripping beds, sorting, washing, drying, folding and returning linen to the property.
  • Separate incompatible items. Batch only items whose colours, soil level and care labels can safely share a cycle.
  • Plan a backup. Decide in advance what happens when a machine, cleaner or usual laundry window is unavailable.

Choose how each load will be handled

Self-service keeps sorting and cycle choices under your control. Wash & Fold reduces the hands-on steps after a store confirms the booking. For Wash & Fold, call the chosen store ahead, drop off and pay upfront, then collect at the agreed time. Turnaround depends on booking and load.

Keep the system visible

A simple written handover can prevent missed steps when more than one person manages the property. Include where clean and used linen is stored, which items belong together, the relevant care labels and who is responsible for checking the final stock before the next guest arrives.

From our experience at Wash BarWash Bar works with Airbnb and short-stay operators who need a practical laundry option around guest turnovers. The right arrangement depends on the load, booking and the operator’s wider cleaning routine.

Questions to settle before the next busy period

  • How many complete linen sets are available for each bed?
  • Which items need separate cycles or special care?
  • Who confirms that clean stock is ready before check-in?
  • Which Wash Bar store is most practical if extra laundry capacity is needed?
