Self-service or Wash & Fold? How to choose

Both options get the laundry done. The better fit depends on how much control you want, whether you can stay with the load and whether you can book ahead.

Wash Bar Team25 September 2026

Self-service puts the wash cycle in your hands. Wash & Fold lets the Wash Bar team handle the washing, drying and folding after you have booked with a store. Neither is automatically better for every load.

Choose self-service when you want control

Self-service suits customers who want to sort their own items, choose the detergent and machine settings, and move the load between washing and drying themselves.

  • You stay responsible for the load from start to finish.
  • You can separate colours, fabrics or care requirements your way.
  • You can pay by coin, card, Apple Pay or Google Pay.
  • No booking is required for the self-service machines.

Choose Wash & Fold when you want it handled

Wash & Fold suits customers who would rather drop off a booked load and collect it after the store has washed, dried and folded it. Turnaround depends on booking and load.

How Wash & Fold works

  1. Call ahead and book. Choose a Wash Bar location and confirm the booking by phone.
  2. Drop off and pay upfront. Bring the laundry to the booked store and pay before service.
  3. We wash, dry and fold. The Wash Bar team handles the load.
  4. Collect. Return at the agreed collection time. Turnaround depends on booking and load.
From our experience at Wash BarThe choice often comes down to control and availability. Self-service keeps each decision with you; Wash & Fold reduces your hands-on involvement after a store confirms the booking.
