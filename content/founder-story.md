### 2. The Journey Continues

```text
THE JOURNEY CONTINUES
When we stepped off the plane in Melbourne in 2006, we had no idea what our future would look like.

We were two international students carrying a single 20kg suitcase, a few hundred dollars, and a dream of building a better life.

Everything was unfamiliar.

The language was different.
The culture was different.
The food was different.

Most importantly, we had no family here to guide us or support us.

Like many migrants, we were starting from the beginning.

There were days filled with excitement and hope, but there were also days filled with uncertainty. We worked part-time jobs, attended classes, worried about bills, and tried to build a future one small step at a time.

Nothing came easily.

Every opportunity had to be earned.
Every dollar had to be carefully managed.
Every challenge taught us something about resilience.

Looking back, those experiences shaped who we are today.

They taught us the importance of working hard, staying humble, and never taking opportunities for granted.

Those same values became the foundation of Wash Bar.

What started as a simple idea, inspired by our own experiences using laundromats, has grown into a trusted brand across Australia and the United States, serving nearly one million customers.

But the numbers are not what matter most to us.

What matters are the people.

The student doing laundry late at night after a long shift.

The family balancing work, children, and everyday life.

The migrant starting a new chapter in a new country.

The entrepreneur chasing a dream.

Because in many ways, we see a part of our own journey in theirs.

As Wash Bar has grown, we have met countless people who remind us of where we started — hardworking individuals with ambition, determination, and the desire to create a better future for themselves and their families.

Many have asked us how we built Wash Bar.

Many have asked if they could do the same.

The truth is, we did not have all the answers when we started.

We made mistakes.

We learned through experience.

We faced challenges and moments of uncertainty.

But every challenge became a lesson, and those lessons helped us build the systems, knowledge, and experience that support Wash Bar today.

That is why we created the Wash Bar Franchise Program.

Not simply because we wanted to open more stores.

But because we wanted to help others achieve what we once dreamed of achieving ourselves.

We want to support people who are willing to work hard, believe in themselves, and take the opportunity to build something of their own.

We want to share the lessons, systems, and knowledge that took us years to develop — and help others avoid some of the mistakes we made along the way.

For us, franchising is about much more than business growth.

It is about helping people build something they can be proud of.

It is about creating opportunities for families.

It is about supporting entrepreneurs who are brave enough to take the first step.

And it is about building a community of people who share the same values that helped us build Wash Bar from the ground up.

From one 20kg suitcase to multiple locations across two countries and nearly one million customers served, our journey has become far greater than we ever imagined.

If there is one thing we have learned, it is this:

Success is not determined by where you start.

It is determined by your willingness to keep moving forward, even when the path ahead is uncertain.

To every customer who has trusted us, every team member who has worked alongside us, every franchise partner who has joined our journey, and every supporter who believed in us along the way — thank you.

You have been part of our story.

And the best chapters are still to come.

**Happy Customers. Happy Business.**

Dharm & Deval
Founders

Wash Bar Laundromat
```
