# Supporting Our Community

## Source material — client email, unedited

```text
At Wash Bar, we believe businesses have the opportunity to do more than provide a service — they can help bring people together and create a positive impact in the communities they serve.

We love collaborating with creative minds and local talent. If you are a creative studio, artist, dancer, filmmaker, content creator, or university project team, we would love to hear your ideas.

Whether it is a creative project, community initiative, collaboration, or something completely new, we are always excited to support and work with people who bring passion, creativity, and positive energy.
```

## Edited version for web

### Creative collaborations

Wash Bar welcomes collaboration ideas from creative studios, artists, dancers,
filmmakers, content creators and university project teams. Whether you are
planning a creative project or community initiative, the team would love to
hear your idea.

**CTA label:** Share a collaboration idea

### Making a difference

Wash Bar supports Cancer Council Victoria and Cancer Council Australia through
free Wash & Dry fundraising events. Proceeds from these initiatives support
cancer research, prevention and services for people affected by cancer.
