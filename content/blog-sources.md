## Editorial safeguards applied

- Care guidance is presented as a practical starting point, not a medical rule.
- Every stain treatment is subordinate to the item care label and product
  instructions.
- Wash Bar Wash & Fold is described only as washing, drying and folding; no
  specialist stain-removal service is implied.
- No public prices, universal turnaround times, WiFi claims, hygiene-superiority
  claims, discounts or guaranteed outcomes are published.
