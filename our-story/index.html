Our Story

The Journey Continues

From one 20kg suitcase to a Wash Bar community across Australia and the United States.

01

Melbourne, 2006

Starting again

We arrived as two international students with one suitcase, a few hundred dollars and a dream of building a better life.

Everything was unfamiliar. Without family here to guide us, we worked part-time, attended classes, managed every dollar carefully and learned to move forward one small step at a time.

Lessons that stayed

The values behind Wash Bar

Resilience

Every challenge became a lesson in continuing through uncertainty.

Humility

Starting over taught us to value people, hard work and every opportunity.

Possibility

The belief that where you begin does not determine where you can go.

An everyday idea

Built from lived experience

Our own experiences using laundromats inspired a simple idea. The resilience, humility and care learned in those early years became the foundation of Wash Bar.

That idea has grown into multiple locations across Australia and the United States, while the people we serve remain at the centre.

People at the centre

Every load belongs to someone’s day

The student doing laundry after a long shift.

The family balancing work, children and everyday life.

The migrant beginning a new chapter in a new country.

The entrepreneur chasing a dream.

Sharing what we learned

The next chapter

People began asking how Wash Bar was built and whether they could do the same. The franchise programme grew from a wish to share the lessons, systems and knowledge developed along the way.

For us, growth is still about people building something they can be proud of.
