# Wash Bar Redesign - Version 3

A mobile-first, conversion-focused redesign for Wash Bar Coin Laundry using
plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Updated Product Problem

Wash Bar needed a modern and professional website that could support both
walk-in customers and business growth.

The redesign focuses on helping customers quickly find locations, prices,
opening hours, directions, and fast wash-and-fold services, while also creating
clear pathways for commercial laundry and franchise enquiries.

## Client Strategy Translated Into Website Goals

The client asked for a modern and professional look, but the real business goal
is growth. The website should help:

- Get more walk-in customers.
- Promote fast 1-hour wash-and-fold.
- Attract commercial laundry clients.
- Support franchise enquiries in Melbourne and the USA.
- Reduce confusion around locations, services, and pricing.
- Improve mobile task completion.

## Primary Homepage Audience

The homepage is primarily for:

- Walk-in customers who need laundry today.
- Wash-and-fold customers who want fast turnaround.

Commercial and franchise users are still supported, but they are routed through
dedicated homepage sections and enquiry calls-to-action.

## Version 3 Design Direction

Wash Bar is positioned as:

- A friendly local laundry for walk-in customers.
- A fast wash-and-fold option for busy people.
- A reliable laundry partner for businesses.
- A growing franchise brand.

The visual direction uses a dark navy hero, bright blue conversion buttons,
rounded white cards, clear service routing, and a sticky mobile action bar.

## Success Metrics

This redesign should be evaluated by whether it can increase:

- Phone call clicks.
- Direction clicks.
- Wash-and-fold enquiries.
- Commercial laundry enquiries.
- Franchise enquiry submissions.
- Mobile task completion.
