Location Experience Concepts

For client review only.
This page compares two ways customers could explore an individual Wash Bar store.

What we are testing

Imagine you want to visit the Wash Bar Fitzroy store this afternoon.
Find Fitzroy, look at the store photos, check its opening hours and find how to get there.

Try both concepts. We are comparing how the store information is accessed, not the visual style.

Concept A — Dedicated Store Page

Selecting a store opens a dedicated location experience with more space for photos, store information, reviews and future local content.

Concept B — Quick Store View
