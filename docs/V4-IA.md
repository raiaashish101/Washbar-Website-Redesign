# Wash Bar Version 4 Information Architecture

## Homepage section order

1. **Header and navigation**
   Provides persistent access to all major sections and a direct call link for high-intent visitors.
2. **Hero**
   Communicates the primary Wash Bar proposition and routes visitors immediately to location discovery or Wash & Fold enquiry.
3. **Quick actions**
   Serves the four most common visitor goals without requiring the user to scroll or read.
4. **Services (Wash & Fold)**
   Promotes the highest-value service with clear benefits and a direct enquiry action.
5. **Who We Serve**
   Shows every customer type Wash Bar supports, including named commercial clients, to build immediate credibility.
6. **Reviews**
   Builds trust through confirmed, source-attributed customer feedback.
7. **Commercial clients**
   Routes commercial enquiries and confirms Wash Bar's experience with named hospitality and healthcare organisations.
8. **Founder story teaser**
   Introduces the brand's human origin without interrupting practical customer tasks.
9. **Supporting Our Community**
   Presents community values through two confirmed initiatives: creative collaboration and Cancer Council support.
10. **Franchise teaser**
    Routes qualified franchise prospects without making unverified promises.
11. **Locations (tab selector)**
    Lets any visitor compare all six stores and access hours, call, and directions with minimal effort.
12. **Enquiry form**
    Captures all lead types with context-aware field behaviour.
13. **Footer**
    Provides secondary navigation, contact details, social links, and legal information.
